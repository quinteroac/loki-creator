# Reference Role Map

Assign each asset one primary role before writing prompts.
Use `vendor/seedance-2.0/references/reference-workflow.md` as the literal
upstream role-map source, then adapt to Loki cards and local artifact rules.

- Image: identity, product, pose, costume, environment, first frame, last frame,
  style, or endpoint.
- Video: motion, camera, pacing, blocking, timing, gesture rhythm, or source
  clip for edit.
- Audio: rhythm, tempo, mood, ambience, dialogue, music texture, or performance
  timing.
- Text: brief, constraints, script, dialogue, subtitles, or delivery notes.

State what transfers and what must not transfer. Selected cards are executable
only when Loki resolves local artifact paths.
