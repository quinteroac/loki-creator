# Project Features: Loki Agentic Creative Suite

## 1. Creative agent management

- Creation of specialized agents for specific creative tasks.
- Definition of agents as packages of capabilities, skills, and tools focused on a specific purpose.
- Creation of ad-hoc agents to solve specific creative project needs.
- Organization of agents into folders, lists, or collections.
- Visual browsing of available agents.
- Export of agents for reuse outside the original environment.
- Sharing of agents with other users or teams.

## 2. Assisted creative experience

- Conversational interface for working with creative agents.
- Canvas-style workspace to visualize, edit, and organize creative outputs.
- Combined chat and canvas workflow.
- Creative iteration through instructions, revisions, and variations.
- Creative project management from a unified interface.

## 3. Generative creative tools

- Image generation.
- Image editing.
- Video generation.
- Video editing.
- Sound effects creation.
- Voice generation.
- Music creation.
- 3D asset generation.
- Gaussian splat creation.
- Creation of worlds or virtual environments.
- LoRA training for styles, characters, products, or specific creative needs.

## 4. Creative analysis tools

- Image analysis.
- Video analysis.
- Music analysis.
- Audio analysis for sound effects.
- Audio analysis for voices.
- Evaluation of creative materials to guide iterations, improvements, or new versions.

## 5. Local and cloud generative AI support

- Use of locally executed generative AI.
- Use of cloud-based generative AI services.
- Ability to combine local and cloud capabilities within the creative workflow.
- Selection of the generation environment based on the user, project, or creative asset needs.

## 6. Creative integrations

- Integration with Blender for 3D workflows and scene creation.
- Integration with Suno for music creation.
- Integration with Niji/Midjourney for visual generation.
- Integration with Krita for illustration and image editing.
- Integration with Aseprite for pixel art and sprite animation.
- Integration with Canva for graphic design and visual content.
- Integration with Figma for interface design, prototyping, and visual assets.
- Integration with Modal for cloud capabilities.
- Integration with Runpod for cloud capabilities focused on generative AI.

## 7. Target users and use cases

- Creatives who need to centralize generation and editing tools.
- Graphic designers working with images, compositions, and visual variations.
- Game developers who need assets, audio, worlds, characters, or visual prototypes.
- YouTubers and content creators producing images, video, music, voice, and sound effects.
- Musicians and producers who want to generate, analyze, or transform audio material.
- Creative teams that want to share reusable agents and workflows.

## 8. Agent library

- Catalog of available agents for different creative domains.
- Grouping of agents by task type or creative discipline.
- Reuse of agents across multiple projects.
- Discovery of specialized agents based on creative needs.

## 9. Collaboration and reuse

- Sharing of agents between users.
- Export of agents as reusable resources.
- Creation of a personal or shared collection of creative agents.
- Use of agents as workflow blocks to accelerate recurring creative processes.

## 10. Product vision

Loki Agentic Creative Suite is an agent-based creative suite designed to centralize the creation, editing, analysis, and organization of generative content. Its focus is to enable creative users to build and use specialized agents to produce visual, audio, audiovisual, 3D, and interactive projects from a unified experience.
